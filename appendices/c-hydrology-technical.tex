\label{app:hydrology-technical}

The source-aware model in Chapter~\ref{ch:research-b} is conditionally
Gaussian after exAL augmentation. This appendix records the source-indexed
updates and the boundary between exact posterior sampling and the
Laplace--delta approximation. Standard filtering and backward sampling
follow \citet{env-prado2021time}.

\section{Source-indexed MCMC}
\label{app:hydrology-mcmc}

Let \(\mathcal J_0=\{0\}\cup\mathcal J\), where source zero denotes USGS
and \(j\in\mathcal J\) denotes an external product. Before the forecast
origin, source-specific observations enter through their historical selectors.
Within the forecast window, the active selector changes with the products
available at each lead. Unequal agency horizons therefore change the
observation vector; unavailable products are not treated as observations from
a common rectangular panel.

Conditional on \((v_t^j,s_t^j,\sigma^j,\gamma^j)\), all active observations
have Gaussian mean shifts and diagonal conditional covariance. The complete
state path is then drawn by one forward-filtering backward-sampling step.

\begin{algorithmblock}{Source-indexed MCMC sampler}
\label{env:alg1}
Initialize the state path and source-specific latent and likelihood
parameters. At each iteration, draw the state path by FFBS; for every active
source, draw the latent scales from their GIG conditionals, the positive shifts
from truncated-normal conditionals, and the scale from its stated conditional;
update bounded skewness by a random walk on an unconstrained transformation;
and propagate the retained state and source parameters through the horizons
available from that source.
\end{algorithmblock}

\section{Laplace--delta variational approximation}
\label{app:hydrology-vb}

The variational family separates the Gaussian state path, source-specific
augmentations, and low-dimensional scale--skewness blocks. Variational
filtering and smoothing update the state, while \(v_t^j\) and \(s_t^j\)
retain GIG and positive-truncated-normal factors. For each source, a Gaussian
Laplace approximation to the transformed \((\sigma^j,\gamma^j)\) factor and
second-order delta calculations provide the remaining moments.

Thus LDVB approximates the MCMC posterior target rather than sampling it
exactly. Its principal risk concerns tail summaries and interval widths in the
nonconjugate blocks. Convergence requires consecutive joint tolerances for the
objective, states, scales, and skewness coordinates.

\paragraph{Source-specific likelihood parameters.}
\label{env:app:sourceparams}
The fitted exAL scales and skewness parameters describe source-specific
observation models, not predictive-synthesis weights. Comparative forecast
evidence comes from the five-origin score tables in
