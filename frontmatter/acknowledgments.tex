\begin{acknowledgements}

This dissertation incorporates material from four collaborative research
lines. The associated manuscripts use the scholarly name Antonio de Leon and
list the candidate as first author. The chapter-opening notices identify the
corresponding manuscript and collaborators, while
Section~\ref{sec:intro-contributions} distinguishes the joint project
contributions from their methodological antecedents.

Chapter~\ref{ch:research-a} adapts material from \emph{exdqlm: An R Package for
Estimation and Analysis of Flexible Dynamic Quantile Linear Models}, coauthored
with Raquel Barata, Raquel Prado, and Bruno Sans\'o
\citep{env-deleon2026exdqlm}. The \pkg{exdqlm} package metadata also lists
Antonio de Leon as an author. The chapter's software contribution integrates model
construction, posterior computation, diagnostics, forecasting, predictive
synthesis, and reproducibility interfaces. The exAL distribution and the
original exDQLM model and algorithms are credited to the antecedent work cited
in the chapter.

Chapter~\ref{ch:research-b} adapts material from \emph{Bayesian
Quantile-Based Correction and Synthesis of Hydrologic Products}, coauthored
with Raquel Prado and Bruno Sans\'o
\citep{qdesn-DeLeonPradoSanso2026HydrologicProducts}. The author is first and
corresponding author of that manuscript. The joint project develops the
source-aware correction and synthesis model, its variational approximation,
and the San Lorenzo River study.

Chapter~\ref{ch:research-c} adapts material from the working manuscript
\emph{Bayesian Quantile Regression with Deep Echo State Network Features},
coauthored with Raquel Prado and Bruno Sans\'o
\citep{qdesn-deleon2026qdesn}. The joint project develops the single-level and
multiquantile formulations, their posterior computations, simulation studies,
and the GloFAS and PriceFM applications.

Chapter~\ref{ch:research-d} combines material from \emph{Mean-Tilted
Intervals: Short Tolerance Intervals} and \emph{Mean-Tilted Intervals:
Regression and Dynamic Models}, both coauthored with Raquel Prado and Bruno
Sans\'o \citep{rqr-deleon2026short,rqr-deleon2026extensions}. The joint work
develops the mean-preserving and mean-tilted interval targets, the calibrated
profiled tolerance procedure, its empirical assessment and application, and
the proposed regression and dynamic endpoint extensions.

The integrated introduction and synthesis provide the dissertation-level
comparison of inferential targets, update mechanisms, computational
approximations, and evidence boundaries across these projects. The statements
above describe contributions at the level of the jointly authored project
where the manuscript records do not assign individual tasks.

\end{acknowledgements}
