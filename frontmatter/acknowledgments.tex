\begin{acknowledgements}

This dissertation incorporates material from four collaborative research
projects. The associated manuscripts use the scholarly name Antonio de Leon and
list the candidate as first author. The descriptions below state contributions
at the level of the jointly authored projects because the available manuscript
records do not assign every task to an individual author.

Chapter~\ref{ch:research-b} adapts material from \emph{Bayesian Quantile-Based
Correction and Synthesis of Hydrologic Products}, coauthored with Raquel Prado
and Bruno Sans\'o \citep{qdesn-DeLeonPradoSanso2026HydrologicProducts}. The
candidate is first and corresponding author. The project develops the
source-aware correction and synthesis model, its variational approximation, and
the San Lorenzo River study.

Chapter~\ref{ch:research-a} adapts material from \emph{exdqlm: An R Package for
Estimation and Analysis of Flexible Dynamic Quantile Linear Models}, coauthored
with Raquel Barata, Raquel Prado, and Bruno Sans\'o
\citep{env-deleon2026exdqlm}. The \pkg{exdqlm} package metadata also lists
Antonio de Leon as an author. The project contribution integrates model
construction, posterior computation, diagnostics, forecasting, predictive
synthesis, and static shrinkage regression. The exAL distribution and the
original exDQLM model and algorithms are credited in the chapter to the work on
which the software builds.

Chapter~\ref{ch:research-c} adapts material from \emph{Bayesian Quantile
Regression with Deep Echo State Network Features}, coauthored with Raquel Prado
and Bruno Sans\'o \citep{qdesn-deleon2026qdesn}. The project develops the
single-level and multiquantile formulations, their posterior computations, the
simulation studies, and the GloFAS and PriceFM applications.

Chapter~\ref{ch:research-d} combines material from \emph{Mean-Tilted Intervals:
Short Tolerance Intervals} and \emph{Mean-Tilted Intervals: Regression and
Dynamic Models}, both coauthored with Raquel Prado and Bruno Sans\'o
\citep{rqr-deleon2026short,rqr-deleon2026extensions}. The project characterizes
the mean-preserving and mean-tilted interval targets, develops the calibrated
profiled tolerance procedure and its empirical assessment, and proposes
regression and dynamic endpoint extensions.

AI-assisted tools were used in a limited supporting role to help debug selected
code and to review the organization, grammar, clarity, consistency, and
readability of the dissertation. They were not used to formulate the statistical
methodology, derive mathematical results or proofs, identify or evaluate the
scientific literature, independently implement the research analyses, or
generate the reported results. The author reviewed and verified all code
changes, references, mathematical statements, analyses, interpretations, and
final text and takes responsibility for the content of the dissertation.

\end{acknowledgements}
